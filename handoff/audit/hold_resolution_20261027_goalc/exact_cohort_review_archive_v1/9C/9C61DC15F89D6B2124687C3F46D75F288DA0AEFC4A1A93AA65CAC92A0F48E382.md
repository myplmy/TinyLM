# 317347 참고 문헌 조각 원형 보류

원문과 후보에 빈 서지 항목과 아래 네 설명만 남아 있다. 설명의 지시 대상인 책 제목·저자·서지 관계는 이 입력에서 확인되지 않으므로 주인공이나 인접 자료에 귀속하지 않는다. 본문에서 이 고립된 서지 묶음을 분리했고, 설명 원형을 보존한다.

```text
참고 문헌 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  (This book is primarily about Arnold's service on the American side in the Revolution, giving overviews of the periods before the war and after he changes sides.)
 
 
 
 
 
  This book is a comprehensive biography, and goes into great detail about Arnold's part in military operations in Quebec, as well as much of the behind-the-scenes political and military wrangling and infighting that occurred prior to his defection. It also includes detailed accounts of his negotiations with Andre and Clinton.
 
  This book includes a reprint of Arnold's diary of his march.
 
  This book is about Arnold's time in Canada both before and after his treachery.
```
