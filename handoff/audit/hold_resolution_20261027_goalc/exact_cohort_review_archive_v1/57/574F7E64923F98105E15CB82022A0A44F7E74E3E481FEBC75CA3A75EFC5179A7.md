## 식이 비어 있는 효율 계산 블록

```text
A simple formula for calculating efficiency is given here:
Psource = the power output by the voltage source, in watts (W)
I = the circuit current, in amperes (A)
R = the total wire resistance (Rwire1 Rwire2), in ohms (Ω)
Algebraically manipulate this equation to solve for wire resistance (R) in terms of all the other variables, and then calculate the maximum amount of allowable wire resistance for a power system where a source outputting 200 kW operates at a circuit current of 48 amps, at a minimum efficiency of 90%.
The maximum allowable (total) wire resistance is 8.681 Ω.
A common mistake for students to make here is entering 90% as “90” rather than as “0.9” in their calculators.
```

본문의 앞 문장 `The efficiency ... is a function ...`은 완결된 설명이므로 유지했다. 뒤의 “formula is given here”에는 실제 식이 없고, 변수·대수 조작·수치 답이 그 식을 직접 전제로 하므로 이 블록을 분리했다. 소실된 식이나 Rwire 항 사이 연산자를 추정하지 않았다.

## 누락된 회로 그림을 가리키는 도선 크기 문제

```text
What size (gauge) of copper wire is needed in this circuit to ensure the load receives at least 110 volts?
#6 gauge copper wire comes close, but is not quite large enough. #5 gauge or larger will suffice.
Several steps are necessary to solve this problem: Ohm’s Law, algebraic manipulation of the specific resistance equation, and research into wire sizes. Be sure to spend adequate time discussing this problem with your students!
```

회로가 없는 상태에서 회로 도선 크기 답을 독립적으로 판단할 수 없어 질문·답·직접 해설을 함께 보류했다.

