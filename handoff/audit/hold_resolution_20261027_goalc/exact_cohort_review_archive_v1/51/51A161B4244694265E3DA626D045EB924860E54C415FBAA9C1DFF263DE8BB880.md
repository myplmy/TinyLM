# 1161815 원형 보류

학습 본문에서 분리한 댓글 반응 UI 및 inbound pingback 메타데이터. 반응 표시의 원문 순서를 보존한다.

```text
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 2 people
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 2 people
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 3 people
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 2 people
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 1 person
LikeLiked by 1 person
Pingback: String Sociology | Not Even Wrong
```

각 `LikeLiked` 표시는 서로 다른 댓글 사이에 놓인 사회적 반응 UI다. pingback은 원문에서 제목과 사이트명만 표시된 inbound 링크 메타데이터이며 발화형 댓글이 아니므로 분리했다. 댓글 본문, 직접 인용, 이름·작품명과 독립 발언은 유지한다.
