READ IN THIS ORDER
1. the two correlations. Under 0.10 closes per-input visit allocation.
2. remember both are UPPER BOUNDS - the shared base inflates them.
3. the two --pairs lines are seed pairs. They also give a second reading
   of the recursion ruler on each body (cla1 had 0.0006, cla2 had 0.0016).
4. r squared, not r. 3.1 sigma on 1464 crops means almost nothing about
   whether the effect is usable.
5. this does NOT test whether recursion helps. It tests whether the help
   lands on predictable inputs.
