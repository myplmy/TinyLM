READ IN THIS ORDER
1. M1 _wq line. If it says (none) the model was frozen and M2/M3 are void.
2. M2 average reuse. For g8 with 16 middle layers expect about 8 for the
   shared MLP tensors. A reuse of 1 means autocast IS caching and the doc
   estimate was wrong in our favour.
3. M2 converted cast bytes per step against the 1 GB gate.
4. M3 percent against the 5 and 20 percent gates.
5. compare the two models. If mC_d36_ag4 is much worse, tying is paying a
   hidden cast cost we have never counted.
REMINDER  no compile here. M3 is an UPPER bound.

          do not close the axis - they are different quantities.
